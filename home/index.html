How it works

Choose. Scan. Send.

Three deliberate steps move one value from your phone to the field you selected.

  1. 1

    Choose the field

    Right-click the exact password or editable field that needs the value.

  2. 2

    Scan once

    Your phone opens the short-lived encrypted path bound to that field.

  3. 3

    Send one value

    Bring it from your phone's password manager. The desktop receives only what you send.

The gap between passwords and passkeys

Passkeys are the direction. Password fields are still everywhere.

Fill from Phone covers the awkward middle: a strong credential is already protected on the phone, but the computer still needs it and does not need the entire vault.

For password-manager teams

A secure handoff primitive—not another password manager.

The reference extension proves a narrow phone-to-field interaction. The underlying protocol can fit inside an existing trusted mobile client and signed browser extension, extending a vault's reach without cloning the vault onto another endpoint.

One requested field. One authorized value. One consumable delivery. The proprietary technology is available for confidential technical diligence, commercial integration, and licensing.

Review the security model and cryptography

Private by design

The relay moves the envelope. It cannot read the message.

Your phone encrypts the value for the browser extension. The service holds only the opaque envelope long enough to deliver it once, and the extension never submits the form for you.
